## Compliance points

- The NSW HCCC can ban unregistered health practitioners. Its code of conduct prohibits misleading claims.
- Avoid outcome claims ("heals", "weight loss", "rejuvenation") and naming medical conditions.
- The name "Psychedelic-assisted therapies & integration" implies drug use. Consider renaming it, for example to "Integration support".
- Keep "not medical care / not an emergency service" wording, with 000 and Lifeline 13 11 14.
- Google Ads won't run ads for plant-medicine terms.
