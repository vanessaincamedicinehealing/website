What is a sound bath?

In a sound bath, you lie down comfortably while the practitioner plays instruments, and your only task is to listen. There is nothing to learn and nothing to get right. Crystal singing bowls are bowls made of quartz that produce long, resonant tones when they are played.

The shamanic crystal bowl sound bath

This is a 50-minute session with shamanic crystal bowls, offered for rest and reflection. Vanessa brings her Andean training to the space, with respect for nature and the elements.

Vanessa “Yana Kuntur” Black Condor is a Sydney-based practitioner of Inca medicine and Q’ero-inspired energy work. She has seven years of training and experience in South American ancestral medicines, including six years of study with the Apaza family at the Inca Medicine School in Cusco, Peru. She is a co-founder of the Paco Shaman Association, registered in 2022, and is certified by the Waira Qeros Peruvian Paco Shaman Association.

What to expect

You arrive, settle in and lie down, and the session begins. The bowls are played for the length of the session, and you are welcome to keep your eyes closed, rest or simply listen. Please ask when you book about what to bring.

The session is 50 minutes long. The booking page shows the current price and available times.

Who it suits

A sound bath suits anyone who would like an hour set aside to pause, whether you are new to ceremony or already have a practice. You do not need any experience, and you do not need to be able to meditate. If you are curious about shamanic practice in a quiet, simple form, this is a gentle place to start.

Sessions run in Sydney, and online options are available. All listed services and prices on the booking page are for online sessions, so book a consultation if you would like to discuss in-person options.

Frequently asked questions

What happens during a sound bath?

You lie down and listen while crystal bowls are played. There is nothing you need to do.

How long is the session?

The shamanic crystal bowl sound bath is 50 minutes.

Do I need to meditate or have experience?

No. You can rest, listen, or let your mind wander. Beginners are welcome.

What are crystal singing bowls?

They are bowls made of quartz crystal that sing a sustained tone when struck or rubbed with a mallet.

Is it online or in person in Sydney?

Both options are possible. The booking page lists online sessions, and in-person options can be discussed in a consultation.

Is this medical or psychological treatment?

No. A sound bath is a spiritual and cultural practice offered for rest and reflection.
